## Stable-release checks still required

- In-game screenshots of the actual marker on the assigned chair: seat and
  backrest, blood/cross/circle, no unwanted projection onto adjacent furniture.
- Natural seating/standing and subsequent chair ownership in a complete
  four-player Steam round; verify the host/other clients see no marker.
- Installation/removal with the shipped archive and the intended loader build.
- If enabling Mod Manager download: approved Nexus game domain and live
  Vortex/NXM installation of this mod.

No gameplay screenshots are supplied: the prior UI captures did not match the
isolated camera receipts, and the isolated screenshot attempt hit a GPU crash.
